The sonar · the background indicator

Fires per hostile, panned to its angle and pitched up as it closes — but no two pings may sound within SONAR_GAP (0.26s) of each other, lane-wide. That cap is what makes a late stage survivable: measured on stage 07, the scheduler asked for 7.8 pings a second and 2.6 sounded. The take is 0.20s, shorter than the gap, so pings never overlap. Trim is 0.05 — the quietest take on the bus by ~9 dB.
