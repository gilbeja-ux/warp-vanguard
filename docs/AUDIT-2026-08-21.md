# Warp Vanguard — full audit (2026-08-21)

A start-to-end audit of the game at version 1.0.3. Seven parallel reviews covered
the player journey, gameplay and balance, art, audio, story and meta screens,
backend and anti-cheat, and release readiness. Every claim below was read in the
code. The load-bearing bugs were re-verified by hand.

**Verdict:** the game is well-built and close to release. The engineering
discipline is high — fairness laws, determinism, the music contract, the render
pipeline. The gaps are not rot. They are a small set of real bugs, one anti-cheat
hole, a large body of written story that never reaches the screen, and a clear
production-value ladder that is mostly cheap.

Severity key: HIGH = fix before a public release. MED = fix soon. LOW = polish.

## 2. Backend, anti-cheat, and leaderboard integrity

### A-1 (HIGH) — Boss RNG contamination breaks the five boss boards
Confirmed by hand. Boss logic draws score-relevant randomness from `Math.random`:
wave seat angle (`52-bosses.js:317`), whether a lock (`:329`), which color
(`:330`), purple roll (`:333`), latch placement (`:285`), wave timing (`:409`).

`Math.random` is the sim's contaminated stream. The renderer draws from it about
481 times per rendered frame (`60-input.js:787`). The headless verifier renders
nothing. Normal spawns were insulated onto a separate `spawnRng` for exactly this
reason; the boss was never moved (0 uses of `spawnRng` in `52-bosses.js`).

Boss levels submit to a real board (`campId:7`, `61-replay.js:24`, `:375`) through
the campaign verified path, which demands `score === recomputed`
(`submit-run/index.ts:214`). So a rendered boss fight and the verifier's
re-simulation are different fights. Honest boss submissions get rejected 400.

The test suite stays green because it records and verifies both headless, so both
sides are self-consistent and never diverge.

**Fix:** route all boss RNG through a dedicated seeded side stream, the same
pattern as `beatStream`. It moves only the five boss-board sim ids.

### A-2 (HIGH, practical) — Replay-stealing
The `traces` Storage bucket is public and world-readable; the agent fetched a real
top run's trace over HTTPS with no auth (200, 171 KB). Nothing binds a trace to
the identity that recorded it, and `submit-run` does no trace dedupe. An attacker
downloads a top trace, brute-forces `w/h` + `mutators` against the reproducible
sim (the 400 body even returns `recomputed`/`integrity`/`steps` as an oracle,
`index.ts:214`), and resubmits under their own JWT. It verifies true and lands.
On campaign boards a fresh `runId` makes a new row each time, so one attacker can
evict the whole top-100 with someone else's play.

**Fix options:** stamp the stored trace with the recording `player_id` + `w/h` +
`mutators` and reject a submission whose JWT `sub` differs; or dedupe by trace
content hash; or make the bucket private and serve replays by signed URL (the
`LEADERBOARDS-SETUP.md` design; the deployment diverged to public). Also stop
returning `recomputed`/`integrity`/`steps` in the 400 body.

### A-3 (MED) — Weekly ladder mixes mutator runs with plain runs
`mutLive` needs `endless`, which weekly sets. `resetRun` only locks mutators
before a campaign clear. The server stores but never filters them
(`submit-run/index.ts:243`). `oneLife` ×2 is nearly free for the players
contesting the top, so the meta forces mutator stacking.
**Fix:** force mutators off in `startWeekly` (no sim-id impact) or segment the board.

### A-4 (MED) — Boss duels have no anti-stall; boss boards are farmable
Swarm waves respawn forever (`52-bosses.js:405`), swarm kills pay full
combo-multiplied bounties, and combo≥5 self-heals integrity (`72-tick.js:912`).
Refuse to fire pulses and score grows about 1.5k/s without bound.
**Fix:** decay swarm bounty after round N, or make `timeSec` a ranking penalty on boss boards.

### A-5 (MED) — Endless / weekly plateaus at mid-campaign difficulty
`endlessCfg` fully spices at 150s and speed caps at 0.59 after surge 6
(`30-campaigns.js:37`). Computed pressure is about 2.9, near C2L6, far under
C5L7's 8.0. An expert run never has to end, so the ranked week is an endurance
contest. **Fix:** let surges 7+ keep raising density and mix. Ship at a week
boundary so frozen boards stay untouched.

### Lower backend items
- LOW — Name filter is ASCII-only (`\w` with no `u` flag), so Cyrillic homoglyphs are stripped not folded; substring match gives Scunthorpe false positives; no server-side min length. `submit-run/index.ts:62`.
- LOW — Account delete leaves two residual ids: reports the player filed against others, and trace objects if the best-effort purge fails. `my-data/index.ts:129`.
- LOW-MED — The local admin console `/api/act` runs destructive writes with the service key and no auth or Origin check. DNS-rebinding reachable while running. `scripts/admin.js:120`.
- LOW — Tool-assisted traces verify as legitimate. Inherent to input-replay verification; name it as the trust ceiling.
- OP — CI builds the verifier bundle but never runs `verifier:status`. Add a job that fails a release when local ≠ server sim id.

**Verified correct:** RLS deny + public read, service-role-only writes, JWT-derived
identity, weekly freeze from the server clock, delete ordering, no service key in
client or git. `npm test` passes (exit 0, "ALL TESTS PASSED").

## 3. Story delivery — the best writing never reaches the screen

This is the single cheapest high-value upgrade in the codebase. The words are
already written and validated.

### S-1 (HIGH) — Three tiers of authored text are dead copy
- The five verdict epilogue `lines` never draw. `91-briefing.js:513` does `c.line || lines.join(' ')`, and every verdict has a `line`, so `lines` always loses. What the closure disc shows instead is the weakest text in the file: "Good job! The system's economy is now booming!" (`campaigns.js:78`).
- The five campaign `story` paragraphs are read only by the Lane Designer (`editor.js:1086`). No game surface shows them.
- All 40 level `hint` strings ("a dead lane — every interdictor here was left ON PURPOSE") render nowhere.

**Fix:** show the verdict `lines` on the closure disc (a one-line precedence
change; the disc was built with room for them). Put the `story` paragraph on the
carousel disc or a dossier tab. Consider the `hint` as a subtitle on the pre-run
disc.

### S-2 (HIGH) — Briefings are skippable and therefore invisible
The pre-run disc is the pre-warp screen. Both thumbs dismiss it instantly with no
read gate (`72-tick.js:89`). The tap gate is 0.35s while a line's char-fade takes
about 1.2s. A player who does exactly what the screen asks sees zero words of
campaign story. Barks carry no plot by rule, so the whole shipped narrative for a
skipping player is five boss taunts plus the closure discs.
**Fix:** hold the grip-dismiss until the caption's char-fade completes, or keep the line on screen through the warp spool.

### Lower story items
- MED — No campaign ends with a hook. A finished contract gets RESTART/MENU, no "NEXT CONTRACT ▸" (qualification gets "FIRST CONTRACT ▸"). `95-menu.js:961`.
- MED — Shown line quality is inconsistent. Terse-ops lines sit beside tour-guide lines ("So far so good! Let's keep it up.", `campaigns.js:55`). Mechanical defects at `:95`, `:150`, `:167`. One line-edit pass fixes it, no code.
- MED — C5 continuity fork on a shown surface: "president … she" (`:234`) vs shown "minister of Xeno Relations" (`:244`); "escort the antidote" (`:243`) is leftover from the old virus-escort fiction.
- LOW — The ranked week has no close countdown. `weekEndMs` exists (`00-core.js:152`). Add "CLOSES IN 2D 14H" on the live rung; stamp folded weeks "FINAL".
- LOW — The dossier omits the contract's two nouns (client, cargo), which are the core of the fiction. Data is in the package.
- LOW — Bark ticker: fade window (`90-hud.js:961`) outlives the clear at `barkHold` 4s (`72-tick.js:250`), so barks hard-cut without their fade. `IN-RUN-VOICE.md` legibility rules are also unmet.

### Naming forks to settle
- "wolf/wolves" appears nowhere in `src/`. Shipped code says "runner" (`52-bosses.js:38`, boot label "AWAITING RUNNER"). PRODUCT.md carries both rules (`:139` vs `:144`).
- `docs/lab/story.json` is a different story than the shipped one — a Renke/Reyes/TRACE mystery serial, acts 2–5 empty. `campaigns.js` is episodic contracts with no shared antagonist. The two disagree about what the game's story is.

## 4. Production-value ladder (art, audio, juice)

Ranked by visible impact per effort, cheapest first.

1. **Combo-scaled kill effects + a first-x10 beat.** At x3–x10 the only signal is a static 14px corner text and zap pitch. The kill streak, rim flash, and burst are byte-identical at x1 and x10. Scale kill-streak/rim intensity with `scoreMul()`; give the first x10 a one-shot ring flourish. `72-tick.js:875-970`, `40-state.js:36`.
2. **Master limiter, and route music through the glue.** No limiter sits over the summed output; `musicGain` bypasses the compressor (`11-music.js:241`) and boss-arrival is "mastered hot, peaks above full scale" (`52-bosses.js:87`). One graph change tames the hot bed and dense-wave stacking.
3. **PERFECT kill distinct flash.** A perfect currently differs only by popup text/color (`72-tick.js:970`). A white rim ping at the zap angle sells the timing skill.
4. **Menu film grain.** The menu is the only state finished without grain, against the "grain + vignette to finish" rule, on the most-stared-at sky. Delete the state test at `99-boot.js:1235`.
5. **Sonar phase vocabulary.** The always-on sonar tick uses one timbre for every threat; a black inverter ticks like a red tap. Map oscillator type/pitch to lock color in the existing `sonarTick(freq, pan)`. `72-tick.js:719`.
6. **Station sun aligned to the world key light ("two suns").** Stations bake under `S3D_LIGHT` (screen-left, ~17° up); planets/rings/enemy plates use `LIGHT_A` (~68° up). The arrival frame pairs them and the companion stands in front of the world. Confirmed in renders. Rotate `S3D_LIGHT`'s azimuth to match `LIGHT_A`, keep the grazing elevation, rebake. `80-tunnel.js:790`.
7. **Fanfare tiers.** NEW BEST and 1/2/3-star all play the same `sfx.arrive()` (`61-replay.js:422`). Layer sting variants keyed off `endStars`, plus a distinct riser for `endNewBest`.
8. **Boss duel music treatment.** The duel gets no music identity. On `spawnBoss`, drop the run track's lowpass to ~2kHz and pull `musicRate` down a few cents, release per shed layer. No new track.
9. **Gate facing variants.** `rotZ` is wired end-to-end and unused; a yawed gate baked through the real pipeline reads better than the face-on one (confirmed in render). Cost is bounded: two baked facings per gate variant (about 360KB + one chunked bake each) plus recording the aperture ellipse for the live layers. Benefit spans 22% of companions plus the survey endpoint.
10. **The 40 briefing-disc keyframes.** The largest missing body of art. Spec is finished (`DISC-ART-SPEC.md`). The destination glam-shot fallback is good, so impact-per-effort is lowest despite being the biggest gap.

### Lower art notes
- Station hull albedo `[126,131,140]` renders mid-grey, well above "matte near-black"; it pairs the brightest metal with the darkest ring at the arrival. Decide it, don't inherit it. `81-station3d.js:348`.
- SPINE reads as cogs at rest (36 cladding boxes per torus); halve and vary pad lengths. `81-station3d.js:656`.
- GATE's baked aperture is a flat teal dish at rest; raise filament contrast or add a centre vortex. `81-station3d.js:368`.

### Audio coverage gaps
- Briefing/typewriter has zero audio. Add a quiet tick per glyph batch (`91-briefing.js`).
- Level unlocks advance silently; no combo-milestone stinger.
- No interruption handler for a phone call. Add a Capacitor `appStateChange` + AVAudioSession observer mirroring the `visibilitychange` handler. `60-input.js:342`.
- The service worker has no audio precache; PWA offline users only have tracks they heard. Fine in the Capacitor shell. `src/sw.js`.
- CREDITS.md owes per-file sfx provenance before store submission (BRAND.md rule).

## 6. Gameplay depth and balance

The fairness engineering is airtight: the booked-arrival ledger, the reachability
law, and the pure linter mirror enforce the 100%-able rule everywhere. The boss
family is one verb across five real fights with thorough telegraphs. The sawtooth
curve is real at four of five seams.

- MED — The sawtooth breaks between C2 and C3. Computed non-boss peaks: C1 1.89 → C2 3.63 → C3 3.17 → C4 5.28 → C5 8.03. The difficulty-3 campaign never exceeds the difficulty-2 peak. Nudge C3L6/L7 past C2L7. Moves those two board ids.
- MED — CHAIN OVERDRIVE is score-negative for score-chasers. A chained kill pays base×0.5 with no combo credit; at combo 10, chain pays 50 where interception paid 1000–2000. Pay chained kills ×`scoreMul()`. `72-tick.js:954`.
- LOW — C1L7 stacks phase-lock debut + bursts + 80% pressure at once, the same pattern the L2 onboarding note warns against. Split the lock intro across L6/L7.
- LOW — Dead knob `crawlers`: rolled every normal spawn and discarded, though `en.drift` support exists (`72-tick.js:717`). Deriving drift from the already-drawn `spin` costs zero extra RNG draws and gives a new read (a target that walks the rim).
- LOW — Dead body `noCharge` (purple pressure drone): fully implemented with its own fairness law, one flag from use.
- LOW — Beat vocabulary underexploited: only `wall`/`strip`/`lull` appear; `enemy`/`lock`/`line`/`pickup` beats are built and linted but authored nowhere.
- OBS — Boss tuning near-placeholders: prism's "unequal speeds" tell converges by round 5; the blockade last-stand sweep is a constant that never accelerates. Volley kills don't increment `zaps`, undercounting volley styles in the tiebreak.

**Determinism note:** any change that consumes a main-RNG draw or edits a spawn
table moves that board's sim id. Batch sim-id-moving changes into one release and
redeploy the verifier with it.
