Warp Vanguard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Warp Vanguard is a game. This policy explains the small amount of data it handles, why, and how to have it removed. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

  • There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • Nothing is ever sold, or shared with third parties for marketing.
  • You are never asked for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • The only data that leaves your device is what a leaderboard needs: an anonymous player id, the display name you type, and how a run went.
  • If you never submit a score, no data leaves your device at all.

What is stored on your device

Your progress, settings, best scores and preferences are saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

What is sent to our server, and why

Warp Vanguard has online leaderboards. When a score is submitted, this is sent:

DataWhy
An anonymous player id Generated automatically by the game. It identifies your rows on a leaderboard so your score can be ranked and updated. It is not linked to your name, your email, your device, or any account you hold anywhere else.
The display name you choose So other players can see who set a score. You choose it and you can change it. Please do not enter your real name — see below.
Run results — score, hits, accuracy, stability, duration, which lane The content of a leaderboard entry.
A replay trace of the run's controls Anti-cheat. The server re-plays the run to confirm the score was actually achieved, which is what keeps the boards meaningful. It records control inputs only — nothing about you or your device.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier, or any device identifier.

Choosing a display name

The name you type is shown publicly on leaderboards. Please do not use your real name or any personal information. Names are filtered automatically, and we may remove or reset any name that is offensive or impersonates someone.

Every leaderboard entry carries a report this link. Use it if a name is offensive, contains someone's real name or personal information, or impersonates someone. Reports are reviewed, and a name that draws enough of them may be reset automatically. A report never affects anyone's score.

Why we are allowed to hold it

We rely on our legitimate interest in running a public, competitive leaderboard. A board only works if it is public and if entries stay put, and that is what a player is doing when they submit a score. We do not ask you to consent to anything, because there is nothing here you would be consenting to beyond the leaderboard itself.

You can object to this at any time, and you do not need a reason. The two controls below are how — see Your controls.

Who processes it

Leaderboard data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, acting on our instructions under a data processing agreement. It is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Like any web service, Supabase records technical request logs that include IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. We do not use those logs to build any profile of you, they are not joined to your leaderboard entries, and they age out on the provider's own schedule.

How long it is kept

Leaderboard entries are kept indefinitely — that is the point of a leaderboard, and weekly boards are deliberately frozen when their week closes so results stand as a permanent record. There is no automatic expiry. What there is instead is a way for you to remove your own entries whenever you like.

Your controls

Both of these live in the game, under MY DATA — on the leaderboard screen, and in System Config. Neither asks you to prove who you are, because your device already holds the only identity involved.

ControlWhat it does
Rename my runs Changes the display name on every entry you hold, on every board, at once. Your scores and their positions are untouched. If you want your chosen name off the public boards, this is usually the control you want.
Delete my runs Removes every entry you hold from every board, deletes the replay traces that went with them, and deletes the anonymous id itself. It cannot be undone.

Two limits on renaming, so you know why it might refuse. It is once a day — the first change is always immediate, and only a second change within the same day has to wait. And a name that has been reset by moderation stays reset; renaming skips those entries. Deleting them is never blocked.

Deleting your leaderboard data does not affect your local progress or your personal bests — those never left your device. Deleting the app does not delete leaderboard entries, so if you want them gone, use the control before you uninstall.

If you can no longer use the controls

Email gilbeja.int@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”. Please be aware of a real limit: entries are not linked to any name, email or account we can look you up by, so without the id held on your device we may not be able to identify which entries are yours. A display name is not proof — anyone can read one off a public board. Where we can act, we will, and we aim to respond within 30 days.

We will always act on a report that an entry contains someone's real name or other personal information, or that a name is offensive or impersonates someone — those we remove or reset without needing to establish who is asking.

Children

Warp Vanguard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The only free-text field in the game is a display name; if you believe a child has entered personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published here and the date at the top will change.
