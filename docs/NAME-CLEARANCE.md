# Name clearance — WARP VANGUARD

Screening run 2026-08-21 (H-09). Web-based search, not an attorney search.
Sources: Google Play store search, iTunes Search API, Steam store search,
itch.io, general web, and USPTO records as indexed by Justia / Trademarkia.

## Verdict

**"Warp Vanguard" is reasonably clear to use as the Play Store title.**
No game, app, studio, or trademark with the exact compound exists on any
store, platform, or register the search reached.

**One standing branding rule:** never use bare "Vanguard" as the standalone
name — not in the store title, not in icons, not in ad copy. The short form
collides at once with Activision's subtitle (Call of Duty: Vanguard), Riot's
anti-cheat (Riot Vanguard), Bushiroad's franchise (Cardfight!! Vanguard), and
The Vanguard Group's famous finance mark. Shorten to "Warp Vanguard" or "WV".

## Exact collisions

None found. Google Play returns "No results". The iTunes API, Steam,
itch.io, and web searches (including WarpVanguard / Warp-Vanguard variants)
return nothing with the compound. No combined mark on Justia or Trademarkia.

## The Vanguard Group

The finance company holds many live VANGUARD registrations, including class 9
for financial software. The class overlap is nominal: game software and
investment software are different goods, and forty years of Vanguard-titled
games coexist with them with no visible enforcement. Not a practical concern
unless the branding ever evokes finance.

## What "Vanguard" alone means legally

No live registration for bare VANGUARD in the video-game classes was found.
The word is a crowded, weak term in games and is only ever protected as
compounds. That cuts both ways: nobody credibly owns it against this game,
and this game's own protection rests on the full compound "Warp Vanguard".

## Caveats — what an attorney search would add

- A full USPTO TESS/TSDR text and design search. This screening used
  web-indexed snapshots; uspto.report blocked direct fetch.
- Phonetic and similarity algorithms that catch marks with no shared word.
- State and common-law (unregistered) rights from small studios.
- Non-US registers: EUIPO, UKIPO, JPO, WIPO Madrid. The Play Store is
  global. A UK mark UK00900921106 "VANGUARD" surfaced and was not analyzed.
- Clearance of the logo and wordmark design, not only the text.
- A re-screen at launch. New applications keep appearing in this space.
