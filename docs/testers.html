Tester Guide

Thanks for testing. You are playing the game before anyone else, and what you send back genuinely changes what ships. This page is short on purpose.

What it is

An arcade game for Android. You guard a convoy through a warp lane — two emitters ride a ring under your thumbs, and everything that wants the convoy has to come through you. Landscape, two thumbs, about a minute to understand.

Getting it

  1. Send me the Gmail address your phone uses for the Play Store. It has to be that exact account or the app simply won't appear for you, with no explanation.
  2. I'll send you a join link. Open it on your phone and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install from Google Play as normal.

If the link says the app isn't available, wait fifteen minutes and try again — Play is slow to propagate. If it still fails, it's almost always the wrong Google account.

Please leave it installed for the whole two weeks. Google requires a minimum number of testers to stay opted in continuously before the game is allowed to launch. Uninstalling early doesn't just remove you — it resets progress toward that requirement and pushes the release date back for everyone. You don't have to play every day. Just don't tidy it off your phone.

A shortcut, so you can see the good bits

There are five campaigns, each ending in a duel with a warp leech — a machine clamped across the lane, drinking it. They are the most interesting thing in the game and they are normally hours away.

So: on a campaign's route map, press and hold the final relay for three seconds. A ring charges around your finger and you drop straight into that boss fight. Each of the five is a different puzzle.

These runs are marked as practice and never reach the leaderboards, so you can't accidentally cheat your way onto a board. This shortcut is removed before public release — it exists for you.

What I most need to know

  • Where did you stop playing, and why? The single most useful thing you can tell me.
  • What was confusing? Especially in the first two minutes.
  • What felt unfair — as opposed to merely hard? Those are different, and the difference matters enormously.
  • Did it run badly? Stutter, overheating, battery, anything.
  • Did anything look wrong on your screen — cut off at the edges, overlapping, hidden behind the notch?

Bugs are welcome, but honest reactions are worth more. "I got bored on relay 6" is more useful than a typo.

If it crashes

Note roughly what you were doing and tell me. Crashes are reported to me automatically, but without knowing what happened they're just a stack trace.

Your data

There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. If you submit a score, it sends an anonymous id, the display name you type, and how the run went — nothing else. Full detail in the privacy policy, and you can delete everything from inside the game under MY DATA.
