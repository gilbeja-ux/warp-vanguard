What Lane Command has on file07 ENTRIES

  • How do I steer?
    CMD

    Hold the phone in landscape, with a thumb on each dial pad in the bottom corners. The left dial runs the blue emitter, the right dial runs the white one. An interdictor collapses when an emitter sits on its bearing at the moment it reaches the ring.

    A controller works too. The two sticks are the two dials.

  • What do the colours mean?
    CMD

    Colour is the rule. Read it before the body reaches the ring.

    • REDSTANDARD. Either emitter takes it.
    • BLUEPHASE LOCKED. Only the blue emitter lands.
    • WHITEPHASE LOCKED. Only the white emitter lands.
    • VIOLETARMORED. Both emitters, docked together.
    • LINKEDBARRIER NET. Two hulls on one line. One emitter on each end.
    • GOLDPULSE CHARGER. Ride it, and it charges you.
  • A stage will not give.
    CMD

    Every stage is completable. Difficulty comes from density and speed, never from a wave you were not meant to survive. Open the stage card and take LANE ASSIST: an eased retry of the same lane. It is unranked, so it never posts a score, and it never costs you anything.

    The FIELD GUIDE on the home screen shows every body you will meet, live.

  • My score did not post.
    CMD

    A score posts at the end of the run and needs a connection at that moment. The server replays every run before it lands on a board, so a posted score can take a few seconds to appear.

    In FREE FLOW the ENDLESS LANE is unranked by design: it never posts. The WEEKLY LANE beside it is one seeded lane, identical for every player, open Monday to Sunday (UTC). When the week closes, its board freezes for good.

  • The game says UPDATE GAME TO POST SCORES.
    CMD

    The server now simulates a newer build than the one you are running, and a score from an older build cannot be verified against it. Update from the store. Your progress, stars and personal bests stay on your device and are not affected.

  • Does it play offline?
    CMD

    Yes. Every contract, every stage and FREE FLOW play with no connection. Only the leaderboards need one.

  • Is anything for sale?
    CMD

    No ads. No consumables. No energy timers. Nothing you could buy will ever move a score. During open testing every contract is free.

Privacy policySTORE COPY ↗

        



Warp Vanguard is a game. This policy explains the small amount of data it
handles, why, and how to have it removed. It is written to be read, not to be
survived.



The short version




    
  
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
    • 
  
  • Nothing is ever sold, or shared with third parties for marketing.
    • 
  
  • You are never asked for your name, email address, phone number or location.
    • 
  
  • The only data that leaves your device is what a leaderboard needs — an anonymous player id, the display name you type, and how a run went — plus any feedback you choose to send us.
    • 
  
  • If you never submit a score and never send feedback, no data leaves your device at all.
    • 






What is stored on your device


Your progress, settings, best scores and preferences are saved locally on
your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.



What is sent to our server, and why


Warp Vanguard has online leaderboards. When a score is submitted, this is
sent:






  
DataWhy

  

    An anonymous player id
    Generated automatically by the game. It identifies your rows on a leaderboard so your score can be ranked and updated. It is not linked to your name, your email, your device, or any account you hold anywhere else.
  

  

    The display name you choose
    So other players can see who set a score. You choose it and you can change it. Please do not enter your real name — see below.
  

  

    Run results — score, hits, accuracy, stability, duration, which lane
    The content of a leaderboard entry.
  

  

    A replay trace of the run's controls
    Anti-cheat. The server re-plays the run to confirm the score was actually achieved, which is what keeps the boards meaningful. It records control inputs only — nothing about you or your device.
  







We do not collect your name, email address, contacts,
photos, location, advertising identifier, or any device identifier.



Feedback you send us


Under System Config there is a Feedback button. It is
entirely optional. If you use it, what you write is sent to us — nobody else ever
sees it, and it is never shown in the game or on a leaderboard.





  
DataWhy

  

    What you type, and which of the four subjects you picked
    The message itself. There is a 600-character limit. Please do not
    include your name, your email address or anything private — the panel
    says so before you send. Nothing comes back down this channel; if you want a
    reply, the panel also shows our contact address, and writing to it is your
    choice rather than something the game does with your data.
  

  

    The version of the game you are running, and the
    stage you last played
    A bug report is not actionable without them, and neither is something you
    could reasonably be asked to type.
  

  

    Your device model and screen size — for example
    “Android 14 Pixel 8” and “844×390” — and, only if the game has just
    crashed, the error message it caught
    Half of all bugs in this game have been one device family's bug, and a
    layout bug is one screen shape's bug. The model is a name millions of
    devices share, read once from your browser. It is not a
    device identifier, nothing is derived from it, and it is never used to
    recognise you or anyone else. No advertising ID, device ID or serial
    is read at any point, and we do not read your language either.
  

  

    Your anonymous player id
    The same one the leaderboard uses. It is attached so that a limit on how
    often feedback can be sent can be applied, and so that Delete my runs
    can find and erase your feedback too.
  






A message written while you are offline is held on your device and sent the
next time the game is online. It is discarded unsent if it is more than a week
old. Nothing else about you is attached, and we do not ask you for a reply
address, because we have no way to write back to an anonymous id and no wish to
hold one. If you want an answer, email us — the address is on the panel and at
the bottom of this page — and then we hold whatever you chose to send us, which
is entirely up to you.



Choosing a display name


The name you type is shown publicly on leaderboards.
Please do not use your real name or any personal information.
Names are filtered automatically, and we may remove or reset any name that is
offensive or impersonates someone.


Every leaderboard entry carries a report this link. Use it if a
name is offensive, contains someone's real name or personal information, or
impersonates someone. Reports are reviewed, and a name that draws enough of them
may be reset automatically. A report never affects anyone's score.



Why we are allowed to hold it


We rely on our legitimate interest in running a public,
competitive leaderboard. A board only works if it is public and if entries stay
put, and that is what a player is doing when they submit a score. We do not ask
you to consent to anything, because there is nothing here you would be consenting
to beyond the leaderboard itself.


You can object to this at any time, and you do not need a reason. The two
controls below are how — see Your controls.



Who processes it


Leaderboard data is stored with Supabase, our hosting
provider, acting on our instructions under a data processing agreement. It is
transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).


Like any web service, Supabase records technical request logs that include
IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. We do not use
those logs to build any profile of you, they are not joined to your leaderboard
entries, and they age out on the provider's own schedule.



How long it is kept


Leaderboard entries are kept indefinitely — that is the point
of a leaderboard, and weekly boards are deliberately frozen when their week
closes so results stand as a permanent record. There is no automatic expiry. What
there is instead is a way for you to remove your own entries whenever you like.


Feedback is different. A message is kept until we have dealt
with it, then for 90 days, and nothing is kept beyond 12 months. You can also
delete yours at any time with the control below.



Your controls


Both of these live in the game, under MY DATA — on the
leaderboard screen, and in System Config. Neither asks you to prove who
you are, because your device already holds the only identity involved.






  
ControlWhat it does

  

    Rename my runs
    Changes the display name on every entry you hold, on every board, at
    once. Your scores and their positions are untouched. If you want your chosen
    name off the public boards, this is usually the control you want.
  

  

    Delete my runs
    Removes every entry you hold from every board, deletes the replay traces
    that went with them, deletes any feedback you have sent us, and deletes the
    anonymous id itself. It cannot be undone.
  







Two limits on renaming, so you know why it might refuse. It is
once a day — the first change is always immediate, and only a
second change within the same day has to wait. And a name that has been
reset by moderation stays reset; renaming skips those entries.
Deleting them is never blocked.



Deleting your leaderboard data does not affect your local progress or your
personal bests — those never left your device. Deleting the app does not delete
leaderboard entries, so if you want them gone, use the control before you
uninstall.



If you can no longer use the controls


Email hello@gb-il.cloud with
the subject “Delete my data”. Please be aware of a real limit:
entries are not linked to any name, email or account we can look you up by, so
without the id held on your device we may not be able to identify which
entries are yours. A display name is not proof — anyone can read one off
a public board. Where we can act, we will, and we aim to respond within 30
days.


We will always act on a report that an entry contains someone's real name or
other personal information, or that a name is offensive or impersonates someone
— those we remove or reset without needing to establish who is asking.



Children


Warp Vanguard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them. There are two free-text fields in the game — the display
name, and the optional feedback message — and both are labelled asking you not to
put personal information in them. If you believe a child has entered personal
information in either, contact us and we will remove it.



Changes


If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published here
and the date at the top will change.
