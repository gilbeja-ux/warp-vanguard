## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored, not category-interchangeable. drawMapList right-aligns every relay row to the lens circle's chord at that row's y — the list arcs against the map rather than sitting beside it. drawMenuHome fits one label size across all sectors then offsets label radius per side so flipped and unflipped labels share a visual centerline.

Caveat: authorship is spent on world fidelity and under-spent on task throughput. Operate mode asks whether the player got where they were going fast and with confidence. These screens answer "yes, eventually, and it looked incredible."

Deterministic scan: 8 findings (7 advisory, 1 warning), ZERO genuine defects in the reviewed surfaces. Three are CSS on the DOM login input, four are in-run gameplay VFX colors, and single-font is a false positive (detector reads only CSS @font-face; DESIGN.md's documented ui-monospace readout voice lives in ctx.font strings it cannot see). The detector contributed nothing here — expected for a canvas product.

No browser overlay: injection into a canvas game surfaces nothing. Real frames were captured via a scratchpad harness instead.

## Overall Impression

Best-executed menu system at this scale, and measurably slower to use than it should be. The primary action for the primary user (resume the campaign) is five taps deep behind two unskippable animations, while the loudest gold top-of-screen element opens a room that is empty by definition on first launch.

## What's Working

1. Locked content is inspectable and the lock states its own remedy — locked relays stay tappable and show "secure the previous relay to decrypt". Converts a wall into a target.
2. The list is shaped by the map it controls — row edges tracking the lens chord make the column unmistakably a control surface for the circle.
3. DEPLOY is the best feedback moment in the product: screen-scale zoom under a 70Hz sub sweep, exactly at commitment.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Five taps to resume — there is no "continue"
Why: PRODUCT.md defines the primary user by short high-replay sessions. Cold launch -> STORY MODE -> spinOut -> carousel -> SYNC STREAM -> discZoom -> relay -> DEPLOY is the highest-frequency path and least optimized. Paid before every run.
Fix: With a campaign in progress, STORY MODE goes straight to the map with frontier selected, labelled RESUME · RELAY 05. Browsing case files becomes a key on the map screen. Carousel stays entry point only for fresh players and post-clear.
Command: /impeccable distill

### [P1] Locked FREE FLOW is invisible, unexplained, and its tap is dead
Why: One third of the home screen. Player cannot tell if it is locked, broken, or unbuilt — and this is where the paywall will land. Verified: unlock reason exists in data and is never rendered; `if (hit && !b.locked)` has no else, so the tap is silent.
Fix: Draw sc.cap on locked sectors, stamp the existing padlock() primitive at the slice midpoint, give locked hits a denial tick plus caption flash.
Command: /impeccable onboard

### [P1] Primary targets below 44px — confirmed independently by both assessments
Why: Relay rows clamped to 20-34px with ZERO hit padding while icon buttons get 6-8px slop. DEPLOY is a hardcoded 34px. Both below platform minimum on every viewport including the 1280x720 capture where the clamp is already maxed.
Fix: Floor row height at 44 and let the column scroll — mapListScroll and its drag handler are already built and do nothing because all rows are forced to fit. Show 6, scroll the rest. Pin the 01-08 numerals at fixed left x.
Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P1] Landmark text renders mirrored inside the lens
Why: Verified three ways — code bakes fillText landmark tags into cityBase, then redraws that sheet twice under ctx.scale(1,-1); magnified crop shows OLD TOWN upside-down with leader line flipped. Reads as a rendering fault and undermines the fidelity every other pixel pays for. Exactly what a store reviewer screenshots.
Fix: Split the sheet — geometry only in cityBase for mirrored passes, landmark tags drawn live in drawMenuMap like relay labels already are.
Command: /impeccable polish

### [P2] Threat glyphs have no legend, and no route to one
Why: The dossier hands the player intel justifying DEPLOY in a vocabulary they cannot read; field guide is gated to home.
Fix: Make the glyph row the target — a button over the cluster opening the guide at that specimen.
Command: /impeccable clarify

## Persona Red Flags

**"Vek" — skill-hungry arcade player (from PRODUCT.md):** Cannot compare his own bests — BEST renders only for the selected relay, so "which relay is my weakest?" means tapping eight rows and holding eight numbers in his head. LEADERBOARD opens empty and offers no path from a relay's scores back into that relay. The frontier gold ring built for him is 1px at 0.6 alpha on a 34px row.

**Two-handed landscape phone player (actual operating context):** On a ~6.1" landscape screen all four chrome keys sit ~77mm from the nearest thumb, including BACK — the most-used navigation control. LEADERBOARD's sector is ~104mm out. The relay list is reach-inverted: relay 01, what a new player wants, is hardest to reach. DEPLOY at ~43mm is the one target respecting the grip.

**Large-thumb / reduced precision:** The hub badge is a false floor — shield renders opaque to ~223px while sectors begin at 142px, leaving an ~80px annulus where the logo visually covers a live hit area.

## Minor Observations

- Destructive wipe sits ~40px from the dev boss-jump hold gesture on relay 08. (RELEASE-PLAN already tracks removing the dev key.)
- TRAFFIC 34 ignores DESIGN.md's own mandated gauge grammar; should be a 5-segment track-and-rails bar.
- Green semantics collide: DESIGN.md reserves Secure Green for secured/cleared/wins. STORY MODE wears it while entirely unsecured.
- Stats split so neither screen is complete: 0/24 stars and 8 RELAYS on the carousel disc, BEST on the map card.
- Three redundant paging cues on the carousel; the clipped disc reads as a layout bug.

## Questions to Consider

1. Why is the wheel a full circle for three slices? Two-thirds of the annulus goes to LEADERBOARD and a locked sector. What would a 180-degree arc across the bottom cost the world, and buy the grip?
2. Has LEADERBOARD earned the top slot and Payload Gold before the player has a single score? The Gold Rule says gold marks a gain; an empty board is not a gain.
3. The paywall lands at relay 04 and relay 04 reads exactly like relay 03. When the seam ships, what says "seam"?
4. What does the lens tell the player that the list does not? If the honest answer is atmosphere, why does it hold 45% of an Operate screen?
